Courtney Ledger grew up outside of Philadelphia. She graduated from the University of Rochester in 1999 where she majored in nursing. Since that time, Courtney has worked with many different patient populations, including pediatrics and adults, in both urban and rural communities, and inside the hospital as well as in primary care. Along the way, she developed a passion for public health, which prompted Courtney to complete a masters in public health with an interest in environmental health and epidemiology.

Courtney landed in the Green Mountain State several years ago and earned a masters of science in nursing from University of Vermont. As a family nurse practitioner, she aims to provide individualized care to patients of all ages to help them achieve their personal health goals. She strives to create a partnership with her patients with the aim of optimizing their health and wellbeing. She enjoys caring for patients of all ages.

Currently, Courtney lives in Burlington with her husband and 2 young sons. She enjoys skiing in the winter and hiking in the summer, canoeing, running, and exploring the hidden corners and local cuisines of VT.
