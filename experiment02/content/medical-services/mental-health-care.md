Maintaining good mental health and wellness is an integral part of your overall well being. When your mental well being is out of balance, it can have a strong adverse effect on one's physical health and we will work diligently with you to avoid or minimize these potential effects.

As part of our routine care for you and your family we will regularly screen for mental health concerns using both subjective data (interviews by the nurses, doctors or nurse practitioners, for example) and objective data (questionnaires that are validated by medical research, for example).

We can treat many common conditions, such as depression and anxiety, as well as substance abuse. When appropriate we will refer out to mental health specialists, including our in house [psychiatric nurse practitioners](specialists) for consultation with medication management, and our LICSW therapist for counseling needs.
