Our office is committed to the overall health of our patients, and that commitment extends beyond the four walls of the exam room. We have designed programs that are free\* to our established patients, that are intended to jumpstart a lifestyle change. Examples of these programs are: Walking groups, Yoga classes, and a 12 Week Nutrition class.

*\*We have found that patients are more successful if there is financial component, so patients are asked to contribute a set amount for each program, which is then returned upon meeting defined attendance criteria. We do not ever want this to be a financial burden however and are always willing to work with patients to determine what will work for their specific needs.*

These opportunities fill quickly, so it is important to add your name to the list. Please [contact our office](contact-us) to receive future notices.
