Leslie Langevin, MS RD CD offers individual and group nutrition counseling to help improve your health and well being. Nutrition counseling is a great way to assess your diet and to receive practical nutrition tips and education to move you towards your goals. At Richmond Family Medicine we work as a team to provide comprehensive care for both medical interventions and lifestyle improvements.

Leslie works with you to find dietary changes that fit your busy lifestyle and can include meal tips and planning, specific nutrition disease education, goal setting and diet analysis. Leslie's philosophy is a "no diet" approach that helps incorporate lifestyle changes that last for the long term instead of seeing only short term success. Leslie also provides nutrition classes with themes incorporating group support and education for weight loss and diabetes.

Some of the conditions that nutrition counseling can help are: diabetes, weight management, GI issues like IBS, IBD, celiac disease, eating disorders, high cholesterol and triglycerides and even sports nutrition for athletes.
