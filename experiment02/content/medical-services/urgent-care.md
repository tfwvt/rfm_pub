We set aside same-day appointments each day for our established patients to give us the opportunity to diagnose and treat acute illness, such as infections, injuries, or worsening of chronic diseases. We also suture small lacerations, treat minor burns, and evaluate and treat injuries such as sprains and minor fractures. The office is also open Saturday mornings for these appointments. A doctor or nurse practitioner is available by telephone after hours for urgent medical issues.

If you are unsure whether you need to be seen immediately, please call and speak with a member of our staff.
