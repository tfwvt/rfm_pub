At Richmond Family Medicine, all patients are asked to select a primary care clinician (either a doctor or nurse practitioner) to ensure continuity of care. If that clinician is unavailable, patients are always able to see any of the other available resources for acute care concerns.

All of the Doctors and Nurse Practitioners are board certified in Family Medicine, and all are trained to care for people of all ages, from newborns to geriatric patients.
