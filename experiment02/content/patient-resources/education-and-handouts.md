There are many printed materials we share with our patients on a daily basis to help in the prevention and treatment of injuries, illness and disease. If you prefer to conserve paper, or have lost a copy of a handout we provided, check this section of our page to find the reference materials.

## Tools for effective management of your diabetes

- Use a **[Food & Blood Glucose Diary](files/handouts/food-and-blood-glucose-diary.pdf)** to monitor your diet and sugars
- Use this **[One Page Reference](files/handouts/keeping-control-of-diabetes.pdf)** as a tip sheet for carefully monitoring your diabetes
- Understand the **[Recommended Tests and Clinical Actions](files/handouts/vermont-guide-to-diabetes-care.pdf)** used to help monitor your diabetes
- Additional resources compiled by the State of Vermont are **[here](https://healthvermont.gov/prevent/diabetes/resources.aspx)**

## Tools for maintaining a healthy weight

- Use a **[Food Journal](files/handouts/food-journal.pdf)** to keep track of your caloric intake
- Find **[Inexpensive AND Healthy options](files/handouts/healthy-and-inexpensive-shopping.pdf)** to stock your fridge & pantry
- Understand how to construct a **[Healthy, Balanced Meal](files/handouts/healthy-balanced-meal.pdf)**
- Assess your current state and set some realistic goals for yourself with a **[Weight & Health Profile](files/handouts/weight-and-health-profile.pdf)**

## Tools and information for mental and emotional wellness

- Learn about some of the **[Medicines AND Alternative Approaches](files/handouts/depression-treatments.pdf)** used to treat depression
- Make a **[Plan](files/handouts/depression-self-care-plan.pdf)** for yourself to help improve your emotional, physical and social wellness
- Keep a **[Diary/Log](files/handouts/depression-activity-diary.pdf)** to keep track of your progress in managing your depression
