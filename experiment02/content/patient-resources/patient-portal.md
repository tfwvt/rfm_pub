We are now online with our Patient Portal! Once you've activated your account you can now:

- Request appointments
- See your past & future appointments
- Request medication refills
- Update relevant medical & family history
- Communicate directly with your clinician
- See a summary of your medical chart

To activate your account, call us at **802-434-4123, option #1**.
