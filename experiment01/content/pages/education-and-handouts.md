There are many printed materials we share with our patients on a daily basis to help in the prevention and treatment of injuries, illness and disease. If you prefer to conserve paper, or have lost a copy of a handout we provided, check this section of our page to find the reference materials.

## Tools for effective management of your diabetes

- Use a **[Food & Blood Glucose Diary](documents/Food-Blood-Glucose-Diary.pdf)** to monitor your diet and sugars
- Use this **[One Page Reference](documents/Keeping-Control-of-Diabetes.pdf)** as a tip sheet for carefully monitoring your diabetes
- Understand the **[Recommended Tests and Clinical Actions](documents/VT-Dept-of-Health-Guide-to-Diabetes-Care.pdf)** used to help monitor your diabetes
- Additional resources compiled by the State of Vermont are **[here](https://healthvermont.gov/prevent/diabetes/resources.aspx)**

## Tools for maintaining a healthy weight

- Use a **[Food Journal](documents/food-journal-template-OP-RFM.pdf)** to keep track of your caloric intake
- Find **[Inexpensive AND Healthy options](documents/cheapshop.pdf)** to stock your fridge & pantry
- Understand how to construct a **[Healthy, Balanced Meal](documents/Obesity-Packet-ChooseMyPlate.pdf)**
- Assess your current state and set some realistic goals for yourself with a **[Weight & Health Profile](documents/YourWeightandHealthProfileFINAL.pdf)**

## Tools and information for mental and emotional wellness

- Learn about some of the **[Medicines AND Alternative Approaches](documents/Information_about_depression_July_2010-1.pdf)** used to treat depression
- Make a **[Plan](documents/depression_self-care_action_plan1.pdf)** for yourself to help improve your emotional, physical and social wellness
- Keep a **[Diary/Log](documents/Depression-activity-DIARY.pdf)** to keep track of your progress in managing your depression
